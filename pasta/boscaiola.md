# Pasta boscaiola recipe


## Ingredients

- rigatoni
- pancetta
- peas
- mushrooms
- onions


## Instructions

1. Sauté the sausage
Heat a drizzle of olive oil in a pan.

Add the crumbled sausage and cook until browned.

Remove and set aside, leaving the fat in the pan.

2. Cook the mushrooms
In the same pan, add onion and garlic; sauté until soft.

Add the mushrooms and cook over medium-high heat until they release their water and become golden.

Deglaze with white wine and let it evaporate.

3. Build the sauce
Return the sausage to the pan.

Add the cream (or tomato passata if using the red version).

Simmer gently for a few minutes until the sauce thickens.

Adjust salt and pepper.

4. Cook the pasta
Boil pasta in salted water until al dente.

Transfer it directly into the sauce and toss well.

5. Serve
Finish with chopped parsley and a twist of black pepper.

Optional: add grated Parmigiano Reggiano.
